=== IMPORTANT LICENSING NOTE FOR QBNEX PROGRAMMERS ===
The QBNex runtime that is in your programs (the QIR runner qb-exec, crates/engine/exec, which every compiled
program and the qb compiler itself are a copy of, with the Rust runtime crates/runtime/core + crates/runtime/audio
linked in) contains no GPL or LGPL code.
FreeType, FreeGLUT, GLEW, zlib, miniaudio, stb_vorbis and the other C libraries of the QB64 runtime are no
longer included, so their licenses no longer apply to your programs.
Programs that play MP3, FLAC or Ogg Vorbis audio contain Symphonia (MPL-2.0, see below).
Every Rust crate in the runtime is listed with its license in THIRD_PARTY.TXT.
In all cases, you should distribute the licenses folder with your program.
